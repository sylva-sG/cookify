Drink Recipes

Explore refreshing drink recipes you can easily make at home

Virgin Mojito

Ingredients:

  • 10 fresh mint leaves
  • 1/2 lime
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • ice
  • sparkling water

Instructions:

  1. crush the mint and lime together
  2. add the ice
  3. pour the sparkling water into the mixture
  4. stir gently to combine flavours
  5. garnish and serve

Mixed Berry Smoothie

Ingredients:

  • cup of mixed berries
  • 1/2 cup yoghurt/milk
  • 1/2 cup of water
  • 2 teaspoons maple syrup

Instructions:

  1. put all ingredients in the blender
  2. blend untill smooth
  3. adjust thickness as needed
  4. serve while chilled

African Tea

Ingredients:

  • 2 teaspoons of tea leaves
  • 1 cup of water
  • sugar to taste
  • milk

Instructions:

  1. boil the water with the tea leaves and leave to boil
  2. add milk and leave to boil
  3. remove fron the heat and add sugar
  4. serve while hot
