samosa

About this Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • tspn of salt
  • 2 tspn of oil
  • water
  • 3 medium potaotes
  • 1 cup of green grams
  • 1 tspn of garlic
  • 1 tspn curry powder
  • 1 tspn of chilli powder
  • 2 tablespoon of oil
  • fresh coriander

Method of preparation

In a bowl mix flour, salt, and oil, then gradually add water and knead into a firm dough; cover and let it rest for about 20 minutes. Meanwhile heat oil in a pan, sauté onions, ginger, and garlic until fragrant, then add the mashed potatoes, peas, curry powder, chili powder, and salt, mixing well and cooking for a few minutes before letting the filling cool. Roll the dough into small circles, cut each circle in half, and shape each half into a cone, fill with the potato mixture, and seal the edges with water. Heat oil in a pan and deep-fry the samosas until golden brown and crispy, then remove and drain on paper towels before serving hot. 🥟

smocha

Ingredients

  • 1 bowl of wheat flour
  • 5 smokies
  • 1 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 avocado
  • cooking oil

method of preparation

To prepare smocha, first cook sausages in a pan or grill until fully heated, then warm a chapati on a pan until soft. Place the cooked sausage in the center of the chapati, add a layer of sliced tomatoes, onions, and shredded cabbage or lettuce, then drizzle with tomato sauce, chili sauce, or mayonnaise if desired. Fold the chapati tightly around the sausage and fillings to form a wrap, press lightly on the pan for a few seconds to warm it through, and serve hot. 🌯
