Breakfast

Pancakes

About this Recipe

Pancakes are a beloved breakfast treat enjoyed worldwide, known for their fluffy texture and versatility. Made from a simple batter of flour, eggs, milk, and a leavening agent like baking powder, pancakes can be customized with various mix-ins such as fruits, chocolate chips, or nuts. They are typically cooked on a griddle or frying pan until golden brown on both sides. Pancakes can be served with a variety of toppings, including syrup, honey, fresh fruit, whipped cream, or even savory options like bacon and eggs. Whether enjoyed as a sweet indulgence or a hearty meal, pancakes are a delightful way to start the day.

Ingredients

Dry Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoonof sugar
  • 1 tsp of baking powder
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tspn of melted butter
  • one half tspn of vanilla extact

Method of preparation

In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. In another bowl, whisk together milk, egg, melted butter (or oil), and vanilla extract if using. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and gently stir until just combined, leaving a few small lumps. Heat a lightly greased non-stick pan over medium heat, then pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set, then flip and cook the other side until golden brown. Remove from the pan and serve warm with toppings like syrup, honey, or fruit. 🥞
