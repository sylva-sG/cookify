ugali and nyamachoma

About this Recipe

Ingredients

Dry Ingredients

  • 1 kg of beef
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 kg of maize flour
  • 2 onions
  • 1l of oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic

Method of preparation

To prepare this classic meal, start by grilling seasoned goat or beef over a hot charcoal fire, turning the meat regularly and basting it with salted water until it is charred on the outside and succulent within. While the meat rests, bring water to a boil in a heavy pot and gradually stir in maize flour, vigorously mashing the mixture against the sides of the pot with a wooden spoon to remove lumps. Continue adding flour until the dough is stiff and pulls away from the edges, then lower the heat and let it steam for a few minutes before shaping the Ugali into a mound and serving it alongside the sliced, smoky Nyama Choma
